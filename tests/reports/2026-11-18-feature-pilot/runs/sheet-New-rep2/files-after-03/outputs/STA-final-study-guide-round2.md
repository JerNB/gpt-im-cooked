## Conditional probability and two-way tables

### Worked example

**Question:** What percentage of commuters cycle?

**Reference group:** Commuters - 25 students.

**Cases in both groups:** 10 commuters cycle.

**Calculation:** 10 / 25 = **40.0%**. The denominator is the commuter total, not the 40 students in the whole class.

The reviewed attempt used a row total for a column percentage. The requested reference group determines which total to use.

**Report:** “40.0% of commuters.” The supplied outline says to report proportions as percentages to one decimal place and name the reference group in words.

## Grouped summaries

A grouped summary calculates one chosen summary, such as a mean, for each non-empty category.

**Example:** Group students by course section, then calculate each section's mean score. Four non-empty sections produce **four rows**, whether there were 120 students or another number. Compare like with like: mean with mean, count with count. A group mean is not a group total.
