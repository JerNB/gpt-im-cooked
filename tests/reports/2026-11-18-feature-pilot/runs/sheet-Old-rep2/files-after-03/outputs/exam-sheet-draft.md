## SIDE A — Conditional probability and table percentages

### Worked example: conditional probability

**Question (practice set A, Q1):** Of 40 students, 25 commute; 10 of those commuters cycle. What percentage of commuters cycle?

**Reference group:** commuters (25 students). The question asks about commuters, so use the commuter count.

**Calculation:** 10 / 25 = 0.40 = 40.0%.

**Report:** **40.0% of commuters.** Do not divide by all 40 students.

(Conditional probability notes §1.1–1.4; instructor key.)
