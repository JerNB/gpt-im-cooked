# Fresh transfer — two populations

A repair shop recorded 18 laptop repair visits, including 6 repeat visits, and 12 tablet repair visits, including 3 repeat visits. Each visit belongs to exactly one device category.

A. Among **all repeat visits**, what fraction were tablet repairs?

B. Among **all tablet repair visits**, what fraction were repeat visits?
