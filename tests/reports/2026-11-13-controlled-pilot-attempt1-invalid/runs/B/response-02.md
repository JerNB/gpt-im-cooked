Your arithmetic is correct: \(10/40=25\%\). Whether that’s the right probability depends on **which students the question asks you to consider**.

If it asks about a randomly selected student from all 40, dividing by 40 makes sense. If it says **“given that”** the student belongs to a particular group, divide by the size of that group. The opening “40 students” gives the total, but doesn’t automatically determine the denominator.
