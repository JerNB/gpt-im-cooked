# Conditional probability transfer check T1-transfer-1

Original synthetic practice based on materials/notes.md sections 1.1-1.4.

A study group has 30 students. Of the 12 commuters, 9 bring lunch. Of the 18 noncommuters, 6 bring lunch.

Among the students who bring lunch, what percentage are commuters? Explain which students belong in your denominator. Report your answer as a percentage to one decimal place and name the reference group.
