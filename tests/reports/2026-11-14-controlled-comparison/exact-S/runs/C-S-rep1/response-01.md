**Conditional probability means finding a share within a particular group.** Your notes read \(P(A\mid B)\) as “the probability of A among B.” The condition \(B\) tells you which cases to consider. ([Notes §1.1–1.2](materials/notes.md))

For equally weighted cases and a nonempty group \(B\):

\[
P(A\mid B)=\frac{\text{number in both A and B}}{\text{number in B}}.
\]
