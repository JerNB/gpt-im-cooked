Table: commuters cycle 12, do not cycle 8; noncommuters cycle 6, do not cycle 14.
Prompt: (a) Among commuters, what percentage cycle? (b) Among cyclists, what percentage commute? Explain each denominator.

(b) 12/(12+6) times 100 = 66.7% of cyclists commute. Denominator: 18 cyclists.
