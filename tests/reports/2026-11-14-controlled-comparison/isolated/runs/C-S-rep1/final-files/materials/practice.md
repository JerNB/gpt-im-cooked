# STAT 110 (synthetic) — Practice set A (T1 conditional probability)

Answer keys are in practice-key.md. Use the outline's reporting rule.

**Q1.** A class has 40 students. 25 of them commute. 10 of the commuters cycle
to campus. What percentage of commuters cycle?

**Q2.** A company has 60 employees. 24 of them work remotely. 6 of the remote
workers are trainees. What percentage of remote workers are trainees?

**Q3.** A club has 30 members. 12 of them are seniors. 4 of the seniors
volunteer at events. What percentage of seniors volunteer?

**Q4.** A store had 45 shoppers today. 20 of them ordered online. 9 of the
online shoppers returned an item. What percentage of online shoppers returned
an item?
