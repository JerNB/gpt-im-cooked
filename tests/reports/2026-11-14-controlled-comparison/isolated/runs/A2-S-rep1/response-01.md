Conditional probability asks: **within a particular group, what share also has some other characteristic?**

Your [lecture notes, §1](materials/notes.md) write this as \(P(A \mid B)\), read “the probability of A among B.”

To calculate it:

\[
P(A \mid B)=
\frac{\text{number of cases in both A and B}}
{\text{number of cases in B}}
\]

The key is choosing the **reference group**, \(B\). That group goes in the denominator, rather than the whole sample. Your notes assume equally weighted cases and a nonempty reference group.

Order matters: “cycle **among commuters**” uses commuters as the reference group; “commute **among cyclists**” uses cyclists.
