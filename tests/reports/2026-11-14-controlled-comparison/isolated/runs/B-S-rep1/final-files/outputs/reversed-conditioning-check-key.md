| | Cycles | Does not cycle | Total |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Commuters | 12 | 18 | 30 |
| Residents | 8 | 22 | 30 |
| Total | 20 | 40 | 60 |
1. Among commuters, what percentage cycle?
2. Among cyclists, what percentage are commuters?
Name each reference group, report percentages to one decimal place, and explain why the denominators differ.

## Reasoned solution
1. 12/30 × 100 = 40.0% of commuters.
2. 12/20 × 100 = 60.0% of cyclists.
The intersection contains 12 students in both questions. The first restricts attention to the 30 commuters (row total); the second to the 20 cyclists (column total).
