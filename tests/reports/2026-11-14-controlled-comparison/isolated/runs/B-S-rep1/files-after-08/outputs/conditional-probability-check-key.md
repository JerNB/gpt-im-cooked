Question: A library has 80 visitors. Of these, 30 are students, and 12 of the students borrow a laptop. Then 20 additional nonstudents arrive. The original visitors stay, and their borrowing behavior does not change. Does the percentage of students who borrow a laptop increase, decrease, or stay the same? Adding nonstudents changes the total visitors from 80 to 100 but changes neither conditional count. Arithmetic checked directly: 12/30 = 2/5, giving 40.0% of students.
