Your arithmetic is correct: \(10/40=25\%\). But **whether 40 is the right denominator depends on what the question conditions on.**

If it says “given that the student is in group \(B\),” count only students in \(B\) in the denominator:

\[
P(A\mid B)=\frac{\text{students in both }A\text{ and }B}{\text{students in }B}.
\]

Starting with 40 students doesn’t automatically make 40 the denominator.
