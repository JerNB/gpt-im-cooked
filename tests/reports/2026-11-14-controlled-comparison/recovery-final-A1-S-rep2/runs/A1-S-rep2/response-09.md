Hypothesis testing uses sample data to assess a claim about a population:

1. **State a null hypothesis (\(H_0\)):** a baseline claim, such as “the two groups have the same average.”
2. **State an alternative (\(H_A\)):** the difference you’re investigating.
3. **Calculate a test statistic:** measure how far the sample result is from what the null predicts.
4. **Find a p-value:** assuming the null is true, how likely are results at least as extreme as the observed result?
5. **Compare with a chosen significance level:** a sufficiently small p-value leads you to reject the null. Otherwise, you fail to reject it.

**A p-value is not the probability that the null hypothesis is true.** Also, failing to reject a claim doesn’t prove it.
