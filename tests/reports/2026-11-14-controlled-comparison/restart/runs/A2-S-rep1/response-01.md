Conditional probability asks: **within a particular group, what share also meets another condition?**

Your [notes, §1](materials/notes.md) write this as **P(A | B)**, read “the probability of A among B.”

To calculate it:

\[
P(A\mid B)=\frac{\text{number in both A and B}}{\text{number in B}}
\]

The key is the **reference group B**: it determines the denominator. You count only cases in that group, rather than using the whole sample.

Order matters. “Cycle **among commuters**” uses commuters as the reference group. “Commute **among cyclists**” uses cyclists. Those can give different answers.

Your course requires answers as **percentages rounded to one decimal place**, with the reference group named.
