Conditional probability asks: **within a particular group, what share have the property we’re interested in?**

Your [notes, §1](materials/notes.md) write this as \(P(A\mid B)\), read “the probability of A **among B**.”

To calculate it:

- **Denominator:** the number of cases in group B—the reference group.
- **Numerator:** the number of cases in that group that also belong to A.

The key is to identify the reference group **before calculating**. Once the question restricts you to B, use B’s total rather than the whole sample.

Order matters: “A among B” and “B among A” generally give different results because their reference groups differ.
