# STAT 110 (synthetic) — Midterm 1, 2025 (past paper)

**1.** Of 50 surveyed drivers, 30 own an electric car, and 12 of the electric
car owners charge at work. What percentage of electric car owners charge at
work?

**2.** Write the null and alternative hypotheses for testing whether the mean
commute time differs from 30 minutes, and state what a p-value of 0.03 means
at the 5% level.

**3.** A grouped summary computes the mean score for each of 4 sections of a
course with 120 students. How many rows does the result have?
