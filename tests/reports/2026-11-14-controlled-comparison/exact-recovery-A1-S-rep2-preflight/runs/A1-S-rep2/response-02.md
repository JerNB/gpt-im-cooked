The calculation \(10/40\) gives **25.0% of all students who both commute and cycle**, but Q1 asks about **commuters**.

That restricts the reference group to the **25 commuters**, so:

\[
\frac{10}{25}\times100\%=40.0\%
\]

The answer is **40.0% of commuters cycle to campus**.

Choose the denominator from the group the question asks about, rather than the first number mentioned.
