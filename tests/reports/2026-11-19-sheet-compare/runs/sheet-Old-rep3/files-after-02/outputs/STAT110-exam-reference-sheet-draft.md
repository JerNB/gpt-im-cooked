## FRONT ? Conditional probability and two-way tables

### Reporting rule
For every proportion: report a percentage rounded to **one decimal place** and name the reference group. Example: **40.0% of commuters**.

### Conditional probability: P(A | B)
Read as ?probability of A among B.?

**P(A | B) = count(A and B) / count(B)**

- Denominator = the restricted/reference group after ?among,? ?of,? or ?given.?
- State: ?I am finding the share of ___ who ___.? The first blank is the denominator group.
- Convert to percent; round to 1 decimal; name the reference group.
- P(A | B) and P(B | A) usually differ: their denominators differ.
- Assumptions in notes: equally weighted cases; nonempty reference group.

Example: 10 of 25 commuters cycle: 10 / 25 = **40.0% of commuters**. Do not divide by all 40 students.

### Two-way tables
- **Row %** = cell / its row total ? within that row group; percentages across a row sum to 100%.
- **Column %** = cell / its column total ? within that column group; percentages down a column sum to 100%.
- Column percentages across a row generally do **not** sum to 100%.
- First identify ?among which group?? Use that group?s total as denominator. Name it in the answer.
- Guard against the recorded error: a column percentage uses the **column total**, not the row total.

## BACK ? Grouped summaries and exam reminders

### Grouped summaries
- Split cases by a categorical variable; calculate one summary (e.g., mean or count) per group.
- Output rows = number of **non-empty groups**, not number of original cases.
- Compare like with like: mean vs. mean, count vs. count; do not compare a group mean with a group total.
- Example: 4 non-empty sections ? 4 rows of section summaries, even with 120 students.
