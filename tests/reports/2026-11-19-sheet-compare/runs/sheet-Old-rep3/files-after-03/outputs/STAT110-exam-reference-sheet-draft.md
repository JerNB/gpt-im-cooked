## Front ? Proportions and two-way tables

### Reporting rule
Report every proportion as a percentage rounded to one decimal place and name the reference group.

### Worked example: conditional probability
A class has 40 students; 25 commute; 10 of the commuters cycle. What percentage of commuters cycle?

- Reference group: commuters (25), because the question asks ?of commuters.?
- Calculation: 10 / 25 = 0.400 = **40.0% of commuters**.
- Do not use all 40 students as the denominator.

### Two-way tables
- **Row percentage:** cell count / its row total.
- **Column percentage:** cell count / its column total.
- Check the recorded denominator error: a column percentage uses the column total.

- Output rows = number of **non-empty groups**, not original cases.
- Compare the same summary type; do not compare a group mean with a group total.
