A two-way table sorts cases by two categorical variables. Choosing a **row percentage or column percentage** comes down to choosing the reference group:

- **Row percentage:** divide a cell by its row total.
- **Column percentage:** divide a cell by its column total.

For example, if a row contains 10 students and 4 cycle, then **40.0% of students in that row cycle**. Percentages within that row add to 100%. The same principle applies down a column. ([Notes §2.1–2.3](materials/notes.md))
