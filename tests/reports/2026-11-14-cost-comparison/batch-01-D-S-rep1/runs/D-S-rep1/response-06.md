I’ll give you a question that reverses the reference group, so you can check which denominator the wording calls for.

**Hi student, let's get you uncooked.**

A class has 60 students. Of the 24 who attended tutoring, 18 passed the exam. In total, 30 students passed.

A student claims: “Since **75.0% of tutoring attendees passed**, **75.0% of students who passed attended tutoring**.”

**Is that claim correct?** Calculate the percentage of students who passed who attended tutoring, and explain your denominator. Report one decimal place and name the reference group.
