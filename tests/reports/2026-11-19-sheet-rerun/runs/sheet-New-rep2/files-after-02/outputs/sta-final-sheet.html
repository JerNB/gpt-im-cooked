Conditional probability + two-way table: choose the denominator

Example counts: among 25 commuters, 10 cycle and 15 do not; among 15 noncommuters, 5 cycle and 10 do not. Total = 40. (Notes sections 1-2; practice Q1)

Ask first: Among which group? That group is the denominator.

Among commuters, what share cycle?10 / 25 = 40.0% of commuters. P(cycle | commuter): overlap divided by commuter total.
Among cyclists, what share commute?10 / 15 = 66.7% of cyclists. P(commuter | cycle): same overlap, different reference group.
Row percentageCell divided by its row total. Each row sums to 100%. Example: commuter/cycle = 10/25 = 40.0%.
Column percentageCell divided by its column total. Each column sums to 100%. Example: commuter/cycle = 10/15 = 66.7%.

Takeaway: P(A | B) means A among B: B supplies the denominator. P(A | B) usually differs from P(B | A). (Notes sections 1.1-1.4, 2.2-2.3)
